Set up your diary card

Your app begins blank, so your tracking areas are completely your own.

  1. Open Settings. Add each emotion, urge, behavior, habit, or goal you want to track.
  2. Choose an answer style. Pick a 0–5 scale, yes/no with a note, or a written reflection.
  3. Save your settings. They stay in this browser and can be changed whenever you want.
